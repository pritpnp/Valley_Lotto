How games are rated

A store earns 5% of every ticket it sells, and the lottery pays back what it pays out, so a game earns its box by selling.

Each game's rating out of 100 is how well it sells across Pennsylvania against the typical game at the same price: 100 = at least as well as the typical one, 20 = a fifth as well. Under {{ '%g' % cutoff }}, send it back.

Sales come from PA's weekly prize counts: prizes claimed a day ÷ prizes printed = share of the game sold a day; times tickets printed and price = dollars a day. Prizes left, odds and top prizes are shown for information and don't change the rating.
