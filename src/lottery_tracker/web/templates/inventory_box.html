What do these mean?

Win odds — PA's published chance of winning anything. Fixed for the life of the game; it never moves.

Prizes left — how much of the game is still unclaimed. Falls as the game sells through.

Low prizes — the small, common wins. These are what keep a regular customer coming back, so they're weighted heavily.

Low-prize trend — whether those small prizes are running out faster than the game as a whole.

Jackpot density — a ratio, not a count: the share of top prizes still out there divided by how much of the game is still unsold. Above 1.00× means the big prizes are lasting longer than the game's sell-through would predict — good for a jackpot hunter. Below 1.00× means they were claimed early and the game is picked over. n/s means "not significant": with only a handful of top prizes the number bounces around on chance alone, so it's shown but ignored in the score rather than allowed to swing the decision.
